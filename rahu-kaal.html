Understand the Concept

What is Rahu Kaal?

Rahu Kaal, also called Rahu Kalam, is a traditional daytime period treated cautiously for beginning certain important new undertakings. It is a timing convention within Panchang practice, not a statement that every activity during the period will fail.

Its clock time is location-sensitive because the daylight period from local sunrise to sunset is used in the calculation. A fixed table can be a rough reference, but a city-based calculation is more precise.

Why Rahu Kaal changes by city

Sunrise and sunset vary by longitude, latitude, season and date. The daytime interval is divided according to the traditional weekday scheme, so the resulting Rahu Kaal window moves with local daylight.

What is usually avoided

Traditional practice mainly treats Rahu Kaal cautiously for initiating important new work, journeys or ceremonies. Routine work already in progress is commonly distinguished from a fresh beginning. Customs vary by family and tradition.

Do not read Rahu Kaal in isolation

Rahu Kaal is one timing factor. If an activity is genuinely important, read the whole Panchang and practical circumstances rather than treating a single window as absolute.

Reading principle: Daily Panchang describes general time quality. Use it as context rather than as a guarantee. Medical, legal, financial and other high-stakes choices should be based on appropriate professional and practical evidence.

Common Questions

Rahu Kaal Today FAQ

Is Rahu Kaal the same time every day?

No. The weekday changes the assigned segment, while local sunrise and sunset change the clock time.

Is Rahu Kaal identical in Delhi and Dehradun?

Not necessarily. Their local sunrise and sunset times differ, so city-based timings can differ.

Should all work stop during Rahu Kaal?

Traditional caution is mainly about starting important new undertakings; ordinary ongoing work is usually treated differently.

Does Rahu Kaal occur at night?

The commonly used Rahu Kaal calculation is a daytime window based on the period between local sunrise and sunset.
