Jyotisha Basics

What does Kundli Matching actually compare?

Kundli Matching, or Guna Milan, is traditionally used as one part of marriage compatibility assessment. The familiar Ashtakoota system assigns points across eight compatibility factors for a maximum score of 36.

The numerical score is useful, but it should not be treated as the entire marriage decision. A deeper Jyotisha review can also consider Mangal Dosha, Lagna, the seventh house, Navamsa, Dasha timing and practical compatibility between the two people and families.

Quick MatchUses Moon Rashi and Nakshatra as a fast first screening when exact birth time may not be available.
Full MatchUses birth details to calculate Moon data and add checks such as Mangal Dosha and critical issues.
Beyond 36 PointsA final marriage assessment can include Lagna, seventh house, Navamsa, Dasha and real-life compatibility.

Responsible Interpretation

Use matching as guidance, not a verdict

A low or high score should not automatically decide a marriage. Traditional exceptions, chart strength, timing and real-world compatibility can materially change how a result should be understood.

Use the calculator as a structured first look. For a serious decision, read both charts comprehensively and include practical conversations between the people involved.

Common Questions

Kundli Matching FAQ

What is a good score out of 36?

There is no single score that can responsibly decide a marriage by itself. The breakdown, exceptions and wider charts matter.

Why can Quick Match and Full Match show the same 36-point score?

The core Ashtakoota score is Moon based. Full Match adds other checks rather than necessarily changing that core score.

Is exact birth time required?

Quick Match can work from Rashi and Nakshatra. Full chart analysis benefits from accurate birth time.

Does Mangal Dosha appear in the quick score?

No. A meaningful Mangal check needs fuller chart information.

Should marriage be rejected only because of one Dosha?

No. A complete assessment should consider the whole chart and practical compatibility, not one label.
