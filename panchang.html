Jyotisha Basics

What are the five limbs of Panchang?

Panchang literally refers to five limbs: Vara, Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga and Karana. Together they provide a traditional calendar and timing framework used for daily observance, Muhurta and Jyotisha interpretation.

Because sunrise, Moon position and local time matter, Panchang timings can vary by location. That is why a city-aware Panchang is more useful than a generic daily list.

TithiThe lunar day, based on the angular relationship between Sun and Moon.
NakshatraThe lunar mansion occupied by the Moon, widely used for daily quality and birth-chart interpretation.
Yoga & KaranaAdditional Panchang limbs used with Tithi, Nakshatra and weekday to refine the character of a period.

Common Questions

Daily Panchang FAQ

Why does Panchang differ between cities?

Sunrise, sunset and local timing windows change with location, and some transitions may fall on different local times.

What is the most important Panchang element?

There is no single universal element. Tithi and Nakshatra are prominent, but the five limbs are designed to be read together.

Should I avoid all work during Rahu Kaal?

Traditionally it is mainly treated cautiously for starting important new undertakings; routine ongoing work is a different matter.

Why can a Tithi change during the day?

Tithi is based on the changing angular separation of Sun and Moon, so its transition does not have to occur at midnight.

Is daily Panchang the same as personal Muhurta?

No. Daily Panchang gives general timing. Personal Muhurta can also consider the individual chart and the specific activity.
