Quick Match vs Full Kundli Matching

Quick Match is a Moon-based screening when only Rashi and Nakshatra are available. Full Matching uses birth details to add chart-level factors. Neither mode turns a score into a decision about a relationship.

Score limits

Why 18/36 is not a universal pass/fail rule

The 36-point total summarizes eight Moon-based compatibility categories. A threshold can be used as a traditional screening convention, but the same total can be produced by different Koota patterns. A strong total does not erase a serious concern in a particular factor, and a lower total does not by itself prove that a relationship cannot work.

Read the componentsCheck which Kootas contributed or lost points instead of looking only at the total.
Review unresolved NadiIf a tool cannot determine a reliable Nadi outcome, treat it as requiring manual review—not as zero points or an automatic failure.
Keep practical factors centralMutual respect, consent, safety, values, communication and shared plans are not contained in a Guna score.
Birth data

Full does not mean certain when the time is uncertain

Full Matching can calculate more layers only when the inputs support them. A rounded or guessed birth time can change Lagna, house placement and divisional charts. When time is unknown, use the Moon-based result for what it is and clearly label the chart-level factors as uncertain.

Do not manufacture precision: entering 12:00 merely because the birth time is unknown produces a precise-looking chart from an invented input. Record the uncertainty instead.

Decision sequence

A responsible way to use matching

  1. Verify both names, dates, times, places and timezones.
  2. Use Quick Match only when Moon details are the available data.
  3. Use Full Match when birth data is reliable; inspect the Koota breakdown and chart factors separately.
  4. Resolve any missing or ambiguous factor manually instead of silently treating it as a negative score.
  5. Keep real-world relationship assessment outside the calculator and central to the decision.
