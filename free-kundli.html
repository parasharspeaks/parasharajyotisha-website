Jyotisha Basics

What is a Kundli?

A Kundli, also called a Janam Kundli or Vedic birth chart, is a map of the sky for the time and place of birth used in Jyotisha. The chart organizes planetary placements into signs and houses so different areas of life can be studied in a structured way.

A responsible reading does not depend on one placement. Lagna, Moon, planets, houses, planetary relationships, Yogas, Dashas and current transits are read together before drawing conclusions.

Lagna & HousesThe Ascendant establishes the house framework used to study life areas such as self, education, work and relationships.
Planets & SignsPlanetary placement by sign and house shows the themes emphasized in the natal chart.
Dasha & TimingVimshottari Dasha is one of the major timing frameworks used to study when planetary themes become active.

Free Kundli FAQ

Is the Kundli generator free?

Yes. The core Kundli tool can be used without a consultation payment.

Why is exact birth time important?

Birth time helps determine the Ascendant and house framework. Uncertain birth time can make some parts of interpretation uncertain.

Can one Yoga or Dosha decide the whole Kundli?

No. A whole-chart reading considers strength, houses, planetary relationships, timing periods and supporting factors.

What is the difference between Kundli and horoscope?

A Kundli specifically refers to the birth chart calculated for a person’s birth time and location; “horoscope” is often used more broadly.
