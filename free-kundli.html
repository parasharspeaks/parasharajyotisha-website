Responsible Interpretation

A Kundli is a framework for interpretation

Astrological charts are best used for reflection, pattern recognition and traditional timing analysis. Lagna, planets, houses, Yogas, Dasha, strength and Gochar should be read together rather than reducing the whole chart to one score or label.

Common Questions

Free Kundli FAQ

Do I need an account to generate a Kundli?

No. Use Quick Kundli when you only want to calculate and view the result. An account is needed only for My Kundli features such as saved profiles and official dashboard PDF reports.

Which option should I use?

Use Quick Kundli for one-time viewing. Use My Kundli Dashboard when you want to save the birth profile, return later or download an official PDF report.

Is My Kundli account paid?

No. The account is used for the private dashboard and its saved-Kundli/PDF workflow. The dashboard shows the current PDF allowance.

Are the calculations different between the two options?

They use the same Kundli calculation foundation. The account route adds persistence and report-management features.

Why is exact birth time important?

Birth time helps determine the Ascendant and house framework. If the recorded birth time is uncertain, some parts of a detailed chart interpretation may also be uncertain.

Can one Yoga or Dosha decide the whole Kundli?

No. A responsible reading considers the whole chart, including planetary strength, houses, relationships between planets, timing periods and supporting factors.
