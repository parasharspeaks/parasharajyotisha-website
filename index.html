M. Sharma — Your Guide

Parashar Jyotisha is guided by M. Sharma with a focus on practical Vedic astrology, Panchanga wisdom and spiritual guidance for modern seekers.

The approach is to use Jyotisha as a tool for self-understanding, right timing, spiritual discipline and wiser decision-making, not as a source of fear or blind prediction.

Through the Vedic Panchanga and Joytisha apps, Parashar Jyotisha brings classical Panchanga, Kundli, Dasha, Gochar, Matching and Numerology tools into a clean digital experience.
