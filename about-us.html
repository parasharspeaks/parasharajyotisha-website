Purpose

Why Parashar Jyotisha exists

The aim is to make traditional Jyotisha information easier to explore without hiding the calculation method behind vague claims. The website combines free calculators, daily Panchang, explanatory guides and personal guidance so a user can move from a number or chart to an understandable context.

Astrology on this site is presented as a cultural, spiritual and interpretive framework—not as a replacement for evidence-based medical, legal, financial or emergency advice.

Author & Guide

About M. Sharma

M. Sharma guides Parashar Jyotisha’s astrology content and personal Jyotisha guidance, with a focus on practical Vedic astrology, Panchang, Kundli analysis, timing and spiritual reflection. The website and associated tools are developed to connect traditional concepts with modern software interfaces.

Interpretation philosophyUse the whole chart and timing context rather than making deterministic claims from a single Yoga, Dosha, transit or score.
Technology philosophyShow users what is being calculated, keep the core tools accessible and document important conventions such as location handling and ayanamsa.
