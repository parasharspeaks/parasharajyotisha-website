Unknown birth time: what changes in a Kundli?

A chart can look exact even when its most time-sensitive input is uncertain. Separate stable planetary information from Lagna, houses and divisional factors before interpreting the result.

Why time matters

The sky moves at different speeds

Slow-moving planets usually remain in the same sign across a short uncertainty window. The Ascendant changes continuously and can cross a sign boundary within a relatively short period. House placement follows the Ascendant, while divisional placements can be sensitive to smaller degree changes.

Often more stableMost planetary signs for the date, especially slow-moving planets, subject to checking any same-day ingress.
Needs time checkingMoon degree, Nakshatra/Pada and Dasha balance when the Moon is near a boundary.
Highly time-sensitiveLagna, houses, house lords by Ascendant, Vargas and interpretations derived from those factors.

Boundary testing

A safer workflow for an approximate time

  1. Write down the source of the time: certificate, hospital record, family memory or estimate.
  2. Use a realistic earliest and latest time instead of one convenient midpoint.
  3. Generate charts at both ends and near any Lagna boundary within the range.
  4. Mark which signs, houses, Nakshatra/Pada and Vargas change.
  5. Interpret only the shared factors as stable; keep the rest conditional.

Birth-time rectification is a separate interpretive process and should not be presented as certainty merely because one proposed time fits selected life events. The assumptions and evidence need to remain visible.
