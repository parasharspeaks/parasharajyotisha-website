Know what you are comparing

These are Lahiri sidereal positions from the same calculation service used by our Kundli tool. Tropical zodiac settings, node conventions, location, timezone and a different calculation moment can produce different results. The current service controls the Rahu/Ketu convention; this page does not offer a true/mean-node switch.

Date and time use the resolved city’s timezone, not necessarily your device’s timezone. Check the resolved location after each update. Around daylight-saving clock changes, local times can be ambiguous; verify the intended moment before relying on the chart.
